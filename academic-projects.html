Capstone · DAMO 699

Emergency Department Length-of-Stay Analytics

A full-stack analytics platform over 19 fiscal years of CIHI NACRS data — 10,685 aggregate records representing roughly 175.8 million emergency department visits. Frequency-weighted non-parametric testing across five pre-registered hypotheses identified inpatient access block as the dominant driver of length of stay (10.6 hours for admitted patients against 2.5 hours for non-admitted), alongside a 19-year volume forecast and an original resource-burden index. Built on a React and FastAPI stack and deployed to AWS EC2 through a Dockerised CI/CD pipeline.
